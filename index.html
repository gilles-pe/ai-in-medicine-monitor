Digital Health & AI in Medicine

What can AI do in medicine today, and where are its limits?

The development of AI and its impact on medicine is hard to follow. Here I bring together studies, benchmarks, milestones and related news on that topic. Special focus on the development of medical knowledge, clinical assistance and autonomous workflows. This should help to understand where we are heading or at least where we are, how capabilities are changing, and which questions to ask when considering AI for healthcare delivery or new medical products.

Have a study, a question or a different perspective to share? Please do so, I love to hear from you.
