Topic / subject

Capability
AI task and benchmark performance, not automatically clinical validity.
Clinical Evidence
Clinical evaluation, including early or simulated clinical studies; see test setting.
Real-World Use
Documented routine use in healthcare, independent of evidence for better outcomes.
Discovery
Biomedical discoveries and research enabled by AI.
Context
Historical or related context outside the four core medical AI themes.
Unclassified
Topic has not yet been classified.

Test setting - what was actually tested

Exam / cases
Questions, crafted cases or published teaching cases, without direct live patient care.
Clinician-chat benchmark
Offline, rubric-scored clinician–AI conversations or physician-authored clinical tasks (e.g. HealthBench Professional), often selected from real clinician chats. The tested model answers archived or curated tasks, not patients in a prospective clinical trial; the origin of a conversation does not prove treatment benefit.
Simulated workflow
Agent or diagnostic actions performed in a sandbox, including replayed EHR records.
Patient actors
Trained actors or standardized patients instead of patients receiving real care.
Retrospective records
Previously collected genuine patient information evaluated after the fact.
Prospective patients
Real patients enrolled and tested prospectively under a predefined study plan.
Routine care
Documented ongoing use in ordinary healthcare delivery; not an announced launch or one-off pilot.
Preclinical / animal
Non-human preclinical experiments including animals or phantoms.
Laboratory / research
Laboratory or computational-science benchmarks, not care of patients.
Non-clinical
Method, policy, historical or non-medical context without a patient evaluation.
Setting unknown
The actual testing environment is not sufficiently evidenced.

Study design - how the test was conducted

Benchmark
Standardized questions or tasks scored against a reference, potentially simulated.
Retrospective evaluation
Analysis of an existing dataset without prospective patient enrollment for the test.
Retrospective simulation
Historical cases replayed in a virtual environment; not prospective treatment.
Prospective single-arm
Prospective real-patient study with no concurrent control arm.
Prospective observational
Pre-planned observation of real patients without random treatment assignment.
Randomized simulation
Randomized vignette or actor experiment, not a clinical patient RCT.
Clinical RCT
Randomized controlled intervention study with real patients.
Routine observational
Observational data from a deployed tool in routine clinical care.
Case report / series
Description of one or several patient cases, not a standardized benchmark or controlled trial.
Historical event
Historical announcement, development or award rather than a study design.
Other design
Study or event not captured by the predefined design categories.
Design unknown
Design has not yet been established from methods.

Milestone marks editorial significance, not stronger clinical evidence. Peer review, model scores, real-patient testing and patient benefit are independent.
