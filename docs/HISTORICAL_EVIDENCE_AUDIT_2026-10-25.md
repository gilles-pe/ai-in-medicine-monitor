# Historical medical-AI evidence audit
Audit date: 2026-10-25. First pass of the source-grouped audit, not a blanket audit of the complete repository.
Audit owner/reviewer: ChatGPT source-assisted review. Review status is limited to the exact original Methods/Results passages cited below; no independent experiment replication or guarantee of clinical validity.

## Scope and interpretation
At start of this run: 115 editorial evidence cards; 1 previously labelled classification-reviewed, 114 classification-provisional. Historical duplicates include benchmark points, study cards, overview cards, and milestone tiles referring to the SAME underlying study. Audit each unique source/cohort once, then update linked cards consistently. An approved numeric claim does not imply comparable frontier inclusion; this audit does not alter plotted values, release/measurement dates, milestone flags or patient outcomes.

**Important:** `classificationStatus: reviewed` only means that the relevant setting and design were checked directly against the original article, NOT that it is externally validated safe clinical care. Evidence quality, comparability, statistical meaning and editorial significance remain separate decisions. `validation.status` of original scores is unchanged.

## HealthBench Professional: owner decision resolved (25 October 2026)
**HealthBench Professional (2026),** https://arxiv.org/html/2604.27470v1, Section 3.2-3.3 & Results:
- The pre-existing card `healthbench-2026` is currently tagged `exam_or_vignette`. This is arguably too narrow: paper describes physician-authored, multi-turn ChatGPT for Clinicians conversations, some in good-faith clinical workflows and ~one-third deliberate red-teaming; it is an OFFLINE author-curated benchmark, not prospective deployment or patient outcomes. Primary source reports system score 59.0, base GPT-5.4 48.1; model versus product harness matter.
- **Owner approved** introducing `clinician_chat_benchmark`. Eighteen existing Professional/claimed Professional evidence cards were relabeled without changing any score validation, numerical result, benchmark protocol or mainline status. This remains an offline evaluation: not real-patient clinical trial, outcome evidence or documented treatment safety. The five HealthBench Core track cards were deliberately excluded because they are a separate, broader dataset. Missing-source/vendor claims remain provisional.
- The taxonomy/meaning decision is resolved, but pre-existing `classificationStatus` and score validation remain unchanged pending each source-specific evidence audit.
